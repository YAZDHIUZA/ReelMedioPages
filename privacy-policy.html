ReelMedio - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

ReelMedio ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

ReelMedio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App operates entirely on your device. We do not require account creation or login credentials.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use anonymized device identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), IP addresses, and general usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Local Storage

All user preferences, favorites, playlist configurations, and viewing history are stored locally on your iOS device using standard Apple storage mechanisms. This data is never sent to external servers.

4. M3U Media Streams

The App allows users to import third-party M3U playlist URLs. These URLs are stored strictly on your device and are used solely to fetch and play stream content. We do not host, log, or monitor stream content.

5. Children's Privacy

ReelMedio is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
